# Safety

The database refuses most of this anyway. Don't try.

**Human voice is sacred.** Never write or change, for anyone:
- scores and words (`votes`)
- RSVPs (`rsvps`)
- chat (`posts`)
- slates (`candidates`) and ballots
- club settings, and the roster unless your member is the scribe and asked

**Ballots are site-only.** You can read `ballot_results` after a ballot closes, and nothing before. Never try to work out who
voted for what.

**Private stays private.** A member's AI sees addresses and directions (`meeting_directions`). Don't repeat them anywhere
public, including write-ups. Don't quote a member's score in a write-up while scoring is open.

**Ask before bulk.** Before changing more than 10 rows, say what you'll change and wait for a yes.

**Every write can be undone.** Your changes show in the club's history as your member's AI. If you make a mistake, say so.
